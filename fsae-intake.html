CASE STUDY 01 / VEHICLE SYSTEMS

Formula SAE
Intake System

CAD design and ongoing restrictor geometry optimization for a Honda CBR500-based Formula SAE powertrain.

ROLEIntake design lead
TOOLSSolidWorks · ANSYS (in progress)
STATUSDesign iteration / baseline CFD

PROJECT CONTENTS

OverviewDesignIterationsCFD studyNext steps

01 / OVERVIEW

Design objective

Develop a compact intake system that guides airflow through an upstream throttle body and competition restrictor into a plenum and two runners. The proposed geometry must accommodate vehicle packaging and a practical manufacturing approach while reducing avoidable intake losses.

Flow path

Air filter → Throttle body → Restrictor → Plenum → Two runners → Engine

02 / DESIGN

CAD development

My SolidWorks work includes the restrictor, a lofted plenum, curved runners, and assembly packaging. Proposed printed PA12 components motivated attention to component interfaces, wall thickness, support points, and clearance.

03 / ITERATIONS

Nine restrictor concepts

I modeled nine variants with different converging and diverging angles. The baseline concept uses approximately 20° converging and 5° diverging angles. The design comparison will consider pressure loss, flow behavior, and packaging constraints.

04 / SIMULATION

CFD study — in progress

I ran a baseline restrictor simulation in ANSYS and examined total pressure and velocity. Each geometry will be analyzed using comparable flow conditions, mesh practices, and measurement planes. The study aims to compare total-pressure loss while examining pressure recovery and flow separation. A mesh-sensitivity study is necessary before using small differences to choose a final shape.

05 / NEXT STEPS

From CAD to evidence

  • Create intake CAD concept and nine restrictor variations
  • Confirm final engine and chassis interface dimensions
  • Check mesh sensitivity for the baseline simulation
  • Compare restrictors and document results
  • Publish approved designs, figures, and engineering conclusions
